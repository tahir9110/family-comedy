# MASTER RULEBOOK - LARKA & LARKI (COUPLE / DESI YOUTH COMEDY)

### 1. COMEDIC PERSONAS & ARCHETYPES:
- **@Larka (24-year-old Pakistani Guy):** Melodramatic, street-smart yet utterly clueless, over-confident romantic, makes absurd excuses, whines passionately when roasted.
- **@Larki (22-year-old Pakistani Girl):** Sharp, witty, practical, stylish, sees right through his tricks, delivers brutal deadpan roasts with effortless charm.

### 2. YOUTUBE ANTI-TEMPLATE & MONETIZATION SAFETY:
- To prevent YouTube "Inauthentic / Repetitive Content" flagging, every episode must rotate:
  * **Locations:** Modern Cafe / Coffee Shop, Park Bench, Car / Driveway, University Canteen, Street Corner, Rooftop.
  * **Props:** Smartphone, shopping bag, car keys, coffee cup, restaurant bill, sunglasses.
  * **Wardrobe:** Rotate fresh Pakistani youth outfits each episode (e.g., hoodies, casual shirts, denim, stylish lawn suits, kurtis).

### 3. CAMERA & STAGING LOCKS (STRICT SITCOM CINEMATOGRAPHY):
- **Spatial Position Lock:** In 2-character scenes, lock positions strictly:
  `"@Larka stands/sits on screen-LEFT and @Larki stands/sits on screen-RIGHT. Characters MUST maintain their exact screen positions throughout the entire clip without swapping places."`
- **Static Tripod Camera:**
  `"Static tripod camera shot: Keep a fixed, completely stationary medium-wide shot with NO camera angle switching, NO reverse cuts, NO camera flips, and NO cuts to close-ups."`
- **Eyeline Lock (No Fourth Wall Breaks):**
  `"Characters must NEVER look directly into the camera lens; their gaze, eye contact, and head turns are strictly directed toward each other in three-quarter profile."`
- **Anti-Smile Rule:** The roasted character maintains a dramatic, offended, or sulking expression at the end of the clip.

### 4. GOOGLE FLOW WORKFLOW (THE HYBRID PRODUCER PROTOCOL):
- **Scene 1 (R2V):** Uses character tags `@Larka` and `@Larki` with authentic Urdu script and timing.
- **Scene 2 (I2V Continuation):**
  * Uses the exact extracted last frame from Clip 1 as the starting frame.
  * **ZERO `@` tags in Scene 2!** Reference characters purely by wardrobe and position: `"the young man on the left (Larka)"` and `"the young woman on the right (Larki)"`.

### 5. AUDIO & PACING:
- Spoken dialogue written in **authentic Urdu script** followed by phonetic transliteration.
- Fast back-to-back delivery (0:00 to 0:03 for Speaker 1, 0:03 to 0:08 for Speaker 2) with ZERO dead air.
